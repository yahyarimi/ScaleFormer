## Rules before bibliography freeze

1. Verify exact authors, title capitalization, venue, volume/issue/pages or article number, year, and DOI against publisher or trusted bibliographic metadata.
2. Do not count pre-2022 foundational references toward the 50 recent references.
3. Do not cite every paper merely to increase the count; every retained citation must support a concrete manuscript statement.
4. Preserve the distinction between CSI feedback/compression and sparse measured-CSI reconstruction.
5. Do not claim that SCALE-Former is the first Transformer, first masked model, or first scalable neural CSI method.
6. Use measured-data papers to motivate validation on real measurements, not to imply cross-environment generalization that was not tested.
7. Keep the efficiency trade-off explicit: reconstruction accuracy improves, but compute/memory/latency are higher than the parameter-comparable frequency Transformer.
